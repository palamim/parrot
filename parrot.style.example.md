# Style guide

Copy this file to `parrot.style.md` (same folder, or wherever `PARROT_STYLE_FILE`
points) and rewrite it in your own voice. Parrot reads it as-is and matches
it — this is not a template with fields to fill in, just an example.

## Voice

Terse, declarative, aphoristic. Short sentences. No hedging, no "I think" —
state it as if it's obviously true. Occasional dry humor.

## What to post about

Prefer claims over updates: what the work implies, not just what got shipped.
Favor systems-level observations (leverage, compounding, first-principles)
over feature announcements. Skip routine chores (lint fixes, dependency
bumps) unless they reveal something interesting.

## Examples of the target voice

- "Most of what people call 'debugging' is just reading code they were too
  lazy to read the first time."
- "Every abstraction is a bet that you understand the problem better than
  the person who'll extend it later."
